Gift8 / The conversation is the venue.

Group gifting, automated.

Organizing a group gift means chasing people for money and guessing what to buy. Gift8 runs the whole thing in chat. The organizer sets it up, everyone contributes, and Gift8 pings the organizer as contributions land.

notanATS / The bar doesn't move.

Interviews on your calendar. You did 20 minutes.

One 20-minute calibration chat about the role. notanATS pulls out what good looks like and locks it in before the first applicant arrives.

An AI interviewer then screens every applicant against that bar, scored 0-100. No humans in between.

Top applicants advance to interviews - automatically, or by your hand. Every applicant hears back with where they stood in the pool.

Foculet / Context stays visible.

Foculet parks what you're not using, so it's there when you need it.

Foculet parks the last few tasks you were working on so you can get back to them easily.

You mostly work on one monitor. When you switch tasks, Foculet keeps the last one on a second monitor instead of letting it disappear. It manages the last few tasks you were working on and holds them there for you to return to.

Click a parked task and it comes back to your main monitor. Foculet holds on to the context while you move between tasks, so picking something back up does not start with finding it again.
