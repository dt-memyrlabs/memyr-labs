Gift8

Group gifting, automated.

Gift8 is the consumer experience for group gifting. It keeps the occasion, contributions and next action moving together, without turning a group chat into a project plan.

A Gift8 API key works with Muse and other agents, such as Grok. The first handshake explains Gift8. Then Gift8 asks the agent for the details it needs. The person can stay in the conversation they already use.

notanATS

Screen for the experience you need to build your team.

notanATS is screening infrastructure. It holds the hiring manager’s definition of the work, screens applicants against it and moves the right people to the interview.

Your own agents and apps can tap into that process through the API. The screening layer becomes part of the system you already operate, rather than another queue someone has to watch.

Foculet Open source ↗

Let’s be honest: tabs and multiple monitors cause more chaos than they fix. Foculet parks what you’re not using, so it’s there when you need it.

Foculet is a Windows app with a Chrome extension. The moment you switch away from a window, it takes a snapshot and parks it as a thumbnail on your spare monitor. Click the thumbnail and the window goes back exactly where it was.

Chrome tabs park with a hotkey. You choose which apps never get parked. Everything stays local on your machine. No cloud. No account.

Muse for Chrome In the works

Let Muse work with the tab you already have open.

This Chrome extension gives a Muse chat access to a browser tab when it needs one. The chat asks. Chrome notifies you. You click the extension icon and choose to hand the tab over.

Then Muse and the browser can work together. It is the same basic idea as Claude in Chrome, built for Muse. The handoff is not finished yet.
